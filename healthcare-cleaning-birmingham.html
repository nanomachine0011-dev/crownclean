Who it is for

General commercial cleaning for carefully managed settings.

We support clinics, practices, therapy centres and other healthcare-related premises where clean reception, staff and shared spaces matter. The quote is based on the site's procedures and the exact non-clinical areas requested.

  • Patient-facing and staff areas scoped clearly before work begins
  • Visits discussed around appointments and opening hours
  • Specialist clinical decontamination is not assumed in a standard quote

What is included

Agreed non-clinical cleaning areas.

  • Reception desks, waiting areas and accessible seating surrounds
  • Staff rooms, offices, kitchens and break spaces
  • Washrooms, mirrors, fixtures and touch points
  • Entrances, corridors, floors and accessible internal glass
  • Bins and agreed shared surfaces

Site requirements

Procedures understood before the first visit.

  • Requested rooms and restricted clinical areas identified
  • Access, security, confidentiality and site rules discussed
  • Approved products or process requirements shared in advance

  • Any specialist infection-control requirement assessed separately

FAQ

Healthcare cleaning questions.

Do you provide specialist clinical decontamination?

Specialist or regulated cleaning is not included automatically. Explain the requirement during your enquiry so suitability and scope can be confirmed.

Can you clean while the facility is closed?

Yes, where access and availability allow. Share appointment times, closing times and security arrangements when requesting a quote.

Can you follow our site procedures?

Site rules and suitable product or process requirements should be provided before quoting so the requested service can be assessed clearly.

What details help you quote?

Send the facility type, postcode, requested rooms, floor types, frequency, operating hours, access and any special requirements.
