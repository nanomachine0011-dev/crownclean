FAQ

Deep Cleaning Birmingham FAQs

How much does deep cleaning cost in Birmingham?

Suitable smaller deep cleans start from GBP 79. Larger homes, heavier condition and extras are quoted before booking.

Do you bring cleaning materials?

Cleaning materials and equipment are available. Please mention any product preferences or sensitive surfaces before the appointment.

Is a deep clean the same as end of tenancy cleaning?

They overlap, but end-of-tenancy cleaning is more handover and inspection focused. Deep cleaning is usually a one-off reset for lived-in homes.

Can I choose priority rooms?

Yes. Share the rooms and problem areas first so the quote and time allocation are realistic.
