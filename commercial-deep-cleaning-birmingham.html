Who it is for

Extra detail when routine cleaning is not enough.

Commercial deep cleaning suits offices, shops, shared buildings, community spaces and other premises needing a one-off reset, periodic detail clean or handover-ready finish. We quote around the actual condition and agreed priorities rather than offering a generic checklist.

  • Detailed cleaning focused on built-up dust, marks and neglected areas
  • Scope tailored to occupied, empty or changing premises
  • Clear priorities agreed before the cleaning date

What is included

Detailed cleaning across agreed areas.

  • Thorough dusting of accessible surfaces, edges and fixtures
  • Detailed kitchens, staff areas and washrooms
  • High-touch points, doors, frames and accessible internal glass
  • Floor cleaning and attention to corners and skirting boards
  • Focused cleaning of rooms or areas identified as priorities

Quoted separately where needed

A clear scope avoids surprises.

  • Carpet cleaning, appliances or specialist floor treatment
  • High-level areas requiring specialist access
  • Post-construction debris, hazardous materials or specialist waste
  • External cleaning or inaccessible glazing
  • Additional labour for heavily affected areas identified in advance

FAQ

Commercial deep cleaning questions.

How is a commercial deep clean different from regular cleaning?

A deep clean allows more time and detail for built-up dirt, less frequently cleaned areas and specific priorities agreed during quoting.

Can you deep clean an occupied business?

Often, yes. The practical scope depends on access, furniture, staff activity and whether areas can be made available safely.

Do you handle hazardous or specialist cleaning?

No specialist requirement should be assumed. Tell us about the site condition and any risks so we can confirm whether the requested work is suitable.

What helps you quote accurately?

Photos, floor area, room list, site condition, access, target date and a clear list of priority areas are especially useful.
