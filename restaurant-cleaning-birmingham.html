Who it is for

Cleaning arranged around hospitality service hours.

We support restaurants, cafes and hospitality premises that need customer areas and agreed staff spaces kept clean and presentable. The exact scope is based on service times, access, floor types and the distinction between routine cleaning and specialist kitchen work.

  • Front-of-house presentation and customer areas prioritised
  • Visits discussed around opening, closing and deliveries
  • Specialist extraction or kitchen-system cleaning is not assumed

What is included

Customer and agreed staff areas.

  • Dining floors, accessible surfaces and seating surrounds
  • Entrances, waiting areas and accessible internal glass
  • Customer washrooms, mirrors and touch points
  • Staff rooms, corridors and agreed back-of-house floors
  • Bins and accessible kitchen exterior surfaces where agreed

Scope boundaries

Requirements made clear before work starts.

  • Food preparation times and safe access discussed
  • Equipment, hot surfaces and restricted areas identified
  • Specialist extraction, ducting and grease-system cleaning excluded unless confirmed
  • Waste, stock and chemical responsibilities clarified
  • Deep-clean priorities assessed separately where needed

FAQ

Restaurant cleaning questions.

Can cleaning happen after service?

Yes, where access and availability allow. Share closing times, deliveries and preferred cleaning windows in your enquiry.

Do you clean commercial kitchen extraction systems?

Specialist extraction, ducting and grease-system cleaning is not included automatically. Explain any specialist requirement so suitability can be confirmed.

Can you clean dining and customer washroom areas?

Yes. Front-of-house floors, accessible surfaces, entrances and customer washrooms can form the core agreed scope.

What details help you quote?

Send the postcode, opening hours, dining layout, requested back-of-house areas, floor types, frequency and priority tasks.
