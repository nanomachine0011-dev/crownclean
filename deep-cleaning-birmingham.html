Deep Cleaning Birmingham FAQs

How much does deep cleaning cost in Birmingham?

Deep cleaning starts from £79 for suitable smaller cleans. Larger homes, heavy build-up and extras are quoted before booking.

Is deep cleaning the same as end of tenancy cleaning?

No. Deep cleaning is a detailed home reset, while end of tenancy cleaning is scoped around move-out and handover requirements.

Can I choose priority rooms?

Yes. Tell us which rooms matter most so the quote and time can be planned around your priorities.
