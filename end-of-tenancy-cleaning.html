FAQ

End of Tenancy Cleaning Birmingham FAQs

How much is end of tenancy cleaning in Birmingham?

Suitable smaller end-of-tenancy cleans start from GBP 99. Final quotes depend on bedrooms, bathrooms, condition, access and optional extras.

Do you help with deposit-back or inventory inspections?

We clean to an agreed handover-focused checklist. No cleaner can guarantee a deposit decision, but the clean is planned around the areas usually checked at inventory.

What is the 24-hour re-clean support?

If something is missed, contact us within 24 hours and we'll review it fairly. Where the concern relates to agreed checklist items within scope, we will advise the fairest next step.

Can you add oven, fridge or carpet cleaning?

Yes. Oven, fridge, inside cupboards, internal windows and carpet cleaning can be quoted as optional extras before booking.
