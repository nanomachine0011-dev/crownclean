End of Tenancy Cleaning Birmingham FAQs

How much is end of tenancy cleaning in Birmingham?

Suitable end of tenancy cleans start from £99. The final quote depends on property size, condition, access and extras such as oven or carpet cleaning.

Can you clean at short notice?

Often, yes. Availability depends on the date and property size. WhatsApp is usually the fastest way to send details and photos.

Do you guarantee deposit return?

No cleaning company can guarantee a deposit decision, but we clean to a clear move-out scope and focus on the areas tenants, landlords and agents usually inspect.

Do I need to empty the property?

An empty property is easier to clean thoroughly, but we can quote furnished properties if you tell us what remains inside.
