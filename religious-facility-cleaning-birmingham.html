Who it is for

Thoughtful cleaning for places of worship and community gathering.

We support churches, mosques, temples, gurdwaras and other religious or community facilities across Birmingham. The cleaning scope is discussed with care around building use, services, prayer times, events, access and any areas that require particular respect.

  • Visits arranged around services, events and community activities
  • Site-specific instructions and restricted areas agreed in advance
  • Routine cleaning and one-off support for busy periods

What is included

Cleaning across agreed worship and shared spaces.

  • Worship, prayer and gathering spaces as instructed
  • Entrances, corridors, stairs and community halls
  • Wash facilities, washrooms and high-touch points
  • Kitchens, serving areas and community rooms
  • Floors, bins, accessible surfaces and internal glass

Respectful site planning

Instructions understood before work starts.

  • Service times, event calendars and preferred cleaning windows
  • Footwear, access or room-specific requirements discussed
  • Sensitive, restricted or ceremonial areas clearly identified
  • Trusted on-site contact and access arrangements agreed
  • Extra support for events or seasonal busy periods quoted separately
