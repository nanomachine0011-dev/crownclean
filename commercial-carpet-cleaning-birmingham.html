FAQ

Commercial carpet cleaning questions.

How long will commercial carpets take to dry?

Drying time varies with carpet type, method, ventilation, temperature and conditions. We discuss practical return-to-use timing before booking.

Can you guarantee stain removal?

No. Stains vary by substance, age and carpet type. We can assess visible marks, but complete removal cannot be promised.

Do rooms need to be cleared?

Clear-floor access usually helps. Tell us about furniture and responsibilities so any movement or restrictions are understood before quoting.

What details help you quote?

Send measurements, photos, carpet type if known, floor access, furniture details, preferred date and the time available for drying.
