Who it is for

Communal cleaning for managed buildings and shared residential spaces.

We work with property managers, landlords, block managers and development teams that need shared areas kept presentable for residents, visitors and contractors. Every building is quoted around its layout, footfall and access arrangements.

  • Practical cleaning schedules for occupied shared buildings
  • Attention to first impressions and high-touch areas
  • Clear scope across floors, entrances and circulation spaces

What is included

Shared areas cleaned to an agreed standard.

  • Entrances, internal doors and accessible glass
  • Staircases, landings, corridors and skirting boards
  • Lift interiors, call buttons and high-touch points
  • Hard floors, mats and carpeted circulation areas
  • Shared washrooms or facilities where included in scope

Building-specific scope

Details agreed before cleaning begins.

  • Number of blocks, floors, entrances and lifts
  • Resident access, keys, codes and on-site contacts
  • Priority areas and any recurring problem spots
  • Bin stores, external thresholds or extras quoted if requested
  • Visit records or reporting requirements discussed at quote stage

FAQ

Communal area cleaning questions.

Do you clean apartment block communal areas?

Yes. We can quote shared entrances, stairwells, landings, corridors, lifts and other agreed internal communal spaces.

Can you work with property or block managers?

Yes. Share your building details, access process, required frequency and reporting expectations when requesting a quote.

Can you include bin stores or external areas?

Some additional areas may be quoted where suitable. Include them in your enquiry so the requested scope is clear from the start.

What information do you need?

Send the postcode, number of blocks and floors, entrances, lifts, floor types, preferred frequency, access notes and priority areas.
