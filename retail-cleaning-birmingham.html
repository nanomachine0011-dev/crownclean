Who it is for

Cleaning for spaces where presentation matters.

We support independent retailers, salons, studios, showrooms and other customer-facing premises across Birmingham. Cleaning can be planned around opening and closing times so the site is ready for customers without disrupting trade.

  • Focus on entrances, customer areas and visible surfaces
  • Visits arranged around opening hours where possible
  • Routine cleaning or detailed one-off retail resets

What is included

Retail areas cleaned around your business.

  • Shop floors, entrances, mats and accessible internal glass
  • Counters, customer touch points and display surrounds
  • Fitting rooms, consultation areas or waiting spaces
  • Staff rooms, kitchens and washrooms
  • Floors, bins, dusting and agreed presentation details

Built around trading hours

A scope that respects your premises.

  • Opening, closing and delivery windows considered

FAQ

Retail cleaning questions.

Can cleaning take place before opening or after closing?

Yes, where access and availability allow. Include your trading hours and preferred cleaning window in the enquiry.

Do you clean salons and showrooms as well as shops?

Yes. We can quote a range of customer-facing premises, with the scope adapted to the layout and areas used.

Can you clean around displays and stock?

We agree accessible areas and any restrictions before cleaning. Fragile displays, stock and specialist items should be identified during quoting.

What details help you quote?

Send the postcode, type of premises, approximate size, opening hours, preferred frequency, floor types and priority areas.
