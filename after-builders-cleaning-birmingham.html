FAQ

After Builders Cleaning Birmingham FAQs

When should I book after builders cleaning?

Book once the building or decorating work has finished and tools or materials are removed. Cleaning during active works usually leads to dust settling again.

Can you remove paint or plaster marks?

We can clean light residue where safe and practical, but heavy or cured marks may need specialist removal or snagging by the contractor.

Do you bring cleaning products?

Yes, we bring suitable cleaning supplies. If a surface needs a specific product, let us know before booking.

Can you clean carpets after building work?

Carpet cleaning can be quoted by room if the carpets need more than vacuuming.
