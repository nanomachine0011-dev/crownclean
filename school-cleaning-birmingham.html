Who it is for

Cleaning support for busy learning environments.

We support schools, training centres and education premises that need a clearly scoped cleaning schedule around lessons, staff access and building use. Site rules and access requirements are discussed before work starts.

  • Visits arranged around lessons, clubs and school access
  • High-use classrooms and shared areas prioritised
  • Site-specific safeguarding and security procedures discussed

What is included

Core school cleaning areas.

  • Classrooms, accessible desks, surfaces and floors
  • Corridors, entrances, stairs and assembly halls
  • Washrooms, mirrors, fixtures and touch points
  • Staff rooms, offices, kitchens and break areas
  • Bins and agreed shared learning spaces

School-specific scope

Requirements agreed before cleaning begins.

  • Buildings, blocks, rooms and floor types listed clearly
  • Term-time, holiday and event schedules considered
  • Keys, alarms, security and authorised access agreed
  • Restricted rooms and equipment identified in advance
  • Specialist or regulated cleaning requests assessed separately

FAQ

School cleaning questions.

Can cleaning take place after the school day?

Yes, where access and availability allow. Share lesson, club and closing times so suitable cleaning windows can be discussed.

Can you provide holiday deep cleaning?

Yes. One-off or periodic detailed cleaning can be quoted for school holidays, subject to the requested areas and available dates.

Will you follow our access and site rules?

Yes. Security, authorised access, restricted areas and other relevant site requirements must be shared before work begins.

What details help you quote?

Send the postcode, room list, floor types, washroom count, preferred frequency, timetable, access and priority areas.
