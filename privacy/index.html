Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how DepositReadyClean collects and uses personal information for cleaning enquiries and bookings across Birmingham and the West Midlands.

Business name: DepositReadyClean

Service area: Birmingham and the West Midlands

Contact email: hello@depositreadyclean.co.uk

What Information We Collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, address or postcode, the cleaning service needed, booking details, and any messages you send to us through website forms, Google Ads lead forms, WhatsApp, phone calls, or email.

How We Use Information

We use your information to provide prices, respond to enquiries, arrange cleaning bookings, contact customers, improve our services, and manage advertising leads.

Google Ads Lead Forms

If you submit a Google lead form, we use the details you provide only to respond to your cleaning enquiry and follow up about the service you requested.

Sharing Information

We do not sell personal data. We only share details with cleaners or team members when needed to complete the service, or if required by law.

Data Retention

We keep enquiry and booking details only as long as necessary for customer service, business records, and legal or tax purposes.

Your Rights

You can request access to your personal data, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or ask us to delete your data where we no longer need to keep it.
