Who it is for

Cleaning for high-use fitness and wellbeing spaces.

We support independent gyms, fitness studios, training facilities and member-focused wellbeing spaces. The scope is planned around footfall, opening hours, classes, changing facilities and the surfaces that members use most.

  • High-touch and member-facing areas prioritised
  • Cleaning windows arranged around classes and opening hours
  • Equipment surfaces cleaned only within the agreed scope

What is included

Core gym cleaning areas.

  • Reception, entrances and member circulation areas
  • Workout floors, studio floors and accessible surfaces
  • Agreed equipment touch points and surrounding areas
  • Changing rooms, lockers exteriors, showers and washrooms
  • Staff areas, bins and internal glass as agreed

Facility-specific scope

Cleaning planned around safe access.

  • Opening hours, classes and quiet periods considered
  • Equipment instructions and restricted areas identified
  • Floor materials and suitable cleaning methods discussed
  • Pool, spa or specialist facilities assessed separately
  • Consumables and member cleaning stations clarified in advance

FAQ

Gym cleaning questions.

Can cleaning happen before opening or after closing?

Yes, where access and availability allow. Share opening hours, class times and preferred windows when requesting a quote.

Do you clean gym equipment?

Accessible equipment touch points can be included where suitable and agreed. Equipment instructions and restrictions should be shared before work starts.

Can you clean changing rooms and showers?

Yes, changing and wash facilities can be included in the quote based on layout, condition and required frequency.

What details help you quote?

Send the postcode, floor area, opening hours, member spaces, changing facilities, frequency and priority tasks.
