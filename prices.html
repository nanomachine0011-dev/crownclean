What affects the quote

Why two properties rarely cost the same.

  • Bedrooms, bathrooms and total room count
  • Current condition and build-up
  • Furnished or unfurnished rooms
  • Optional extras such as oven, fridge, cupboards, carpets or internal windows
  • Access, parking, keys and time-sensitive deadlines

How to get a clearer quote

Send the details that matter.

  • Postcode and preferred cleaning date
  • Property type and size
  • Photos of heavy build-up or builder dust
  • Any extras needed
  • Access notes and parking details

FAQ

Cleaning Prices Birmingham FAQs

Are these final cleaning prices?

No. They are guidance only. Final quotes depend on property size, condition, extras, access, parking and timing.

Which prices are confirmed starting points?

End-of-tenancy cleaning from GBP 99, deep cleaning from GBP 79 and oven cleaning from GBP 60 are listed as known starting points.

How are variable cleaning services quoted?

After builders, Airbnb turnover and commercial cleaning are quoted after we review the property details, site size, access, frequency and agreed extras.

How do I get an accurate quote?

Send the service needed, postcode, property size, photos if useful, preferred date and any extras required.

Need cleaning price guidance?

Send your property details and the service you need. We will confirm the scope and quote before work starts.
