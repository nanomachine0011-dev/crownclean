Daily or weekly office cleaning tasks

The right frequency depends on staff numbers, customer visits, kitchens, washrooms and how visible the space is to visitors.

  • Daily cleaning for busy washrooms and kitchens
  • Weekly cleaning for lower-use spaces
  • Periodic deep cleaning for edges and build-up
  • Out-of-hours cleaning where access allows
  • Commercial carpet cleaning when floors need a refresh

Workspace and reception checklist

Office cleaning should protect presentation without disturbing staff equipment or confidential items.

  • Desks and shared worktops where agreed
  • Meeting tables and reception surfaces
  • Door handles and high-touch points
  • Bins where agreed
  • Floors vacuumed or mopped where suitable

Kitchen and washroom checklist

Staff kitchens and washrooms often need the most consistent attention because they are used throughout the working day.

  • Sinks, taps and counters
  • Cupboard fronts and appliance exteriors
  • Toilets, basins and mirrors
  • Touch points and visible marks
  • Consumables checks where agreed

Details to send for an office cleaning quote

A clear quote needs practical site information, especially for Birmingham offices with shared access, parking restrictions or out-of-hours requirements.

  • Postcode and business type
  • Desk areas, meeting rooms, kitchens and washrooms
  • Preferred frequency
  • Access and security notes
  • Whether commercial carpet cleaning is needed

FAQs

Do Birmingham offices need daily cleaning?

Some do, especially where kitchens, washrooms or customer areas are busy. Smaller offices may only need weekly cleaning.

Can office cleaning happen out of hours?

Where access and staffing allow, early morning, evening or closed-period cleaning can be discussed.

Do you provide commercial cleaning beyond offices?

Yes. DepositReadyClean also quotes wider commercial cleaning for Birmingham business spaces.

Can office carpets be cleaned?

Yes. Commercial carpet cleaning can be quoted by area or room.
