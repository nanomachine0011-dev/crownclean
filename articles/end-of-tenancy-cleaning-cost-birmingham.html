End of tenancy cleaning costs in Birmingham depend on more than bedroom count. The final quote usually reflects property size, condition, access, furnished rooms and optional extras such as oven or carpet cleaning.

Starting prices for Birmingham move-out cleaning

DepositReadyClean lists end of tenancy cleaning from GBP 99 for suitable smaller cleans. This is a starting point, not an automatic fixed price for every property.

  • End of tenancy cleaning from GBP 99 where suitable
  • Oven cleaning add-on from GBP 60
  • Carpet cleaning quoted by rooms
  • Larger or heavily used properties need a custom quote

What affects the final quote

Two properties with the same number of bedrooms can take very different amounts of time. Condition, access and extras matter.

  • Bedrooms, bathrooms and total room count
  • Current condition and build-up
  • Furnished or unfurnished rooms
  • Parking, stairs, lifts and access
  • Short-notice deadlines or fixed handover times

Common extras that change the price

Extras should be agreed before booking so the quote is clear and no one is surprised on the day.

  • Oven cleaning
  • Fridge or freezer cleaning if empty and defrosted
  • Inside cupboards
  • Internal windows
  • Carpet cleaning by room

How to get a more accurate quote

A few details help us quote more fairly. WhatsApp is useful because you can send photos quickly.

  • Send your Birmingham postcode
  • Share bedrooms, bathrooms and property type
  • Mention heavy build-up or furnished rooms
  • Confirm access and parking
  • Add photos of areas that need extra attention

FAQs

Is GBP 99 the final price for every property?

No. GBP 99 is a from price for suitable smaller cleans. The final quote depends on property size, condition and extras.

Why does carpet cleaning cost extra?

Carpet cleaning needs separate time and equipment, so it is quoted by room, stairs and condition.

Can I get a quote on WhatsApp?

Yes. Send the postcode, property size, date and photos if useful.

Are quotes confirmed before work starts?

Yes. The scope and quote should be clear before the cleaning appointment.

Want a clear end of tenancy cleaning quote?

Send the postcode, property size, preferred date and photos if useful. We will reply with a clear quote before work starts.
