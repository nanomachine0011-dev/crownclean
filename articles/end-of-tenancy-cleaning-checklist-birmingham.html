Moving out is easier when the cleaning list is clear before the last week. This Birmingham-focused checklist is written for tenants, landlords and letting agents who want a property ready for handover without guesswork.

Before you start the move-out clean

Before cleaning starts, check the tenancy inventory, remove personal items and confirm access, parking and key return times. If a letting agent has shared notes, keep them handy so the cleaning priorities match the inspection.

  • Empty rooms wherever possible
  • Defrost the fridge/freezer if it needs cleaning
  • Take photos of heavy build-up or problem areas
  • Confirm whether oven, carpets or internal windows are needed
  • Leave running water and electricity available

Practical end of tenancy cleaning checklist

A good tenancy clean is usually about details: the kitchen, bathrooms, skirting boards, switches and floors are all areas that tend to be checked closely.

  • Kitchen worktops, sink, taps, splashbacks and cupboard fronts
  • Bathroom toilet, bath, shower, basin, taps, mirrors and tiles
  • Bedrooms, wardrobes exteriors, ledges, skirting boards and switches
  • Living areas, doors, handles, reachable dust and visible marks
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped where suitable

Optional extras to confirm before booking

Some tasks take extra time or need separate equipment, so it is better to mention them before the quote is agreed.

  • Oven cleaning
  • Fridge cleaning if empty and defrosted
  • Carpet cleaning by room
  • Inside cupboards
  • Internal windows where safely reachable

Birmingham handover tips

In Birmingham rental areas such as Edgbaston, Selly Oak, Harborne and the city centre, access and timing can be just as important as the checklist. Share postcode, parking notes and key arrangements early so the clean can be planned properly.

  • Tell the cleaner if there are stairs, lifts or permit parking
  • Share the checkout date and time
  • Mention furnished rooms
  • Send photos if the property has heavy build-up
  • Keep agent notes or inventory issues in one place

FAQs

Do I need to empty the property first?

An empty property is easier to clean thoroughly. If furniture remains, tell us before booking so the quote reflects the actual condition.

No cleaning company can guarantee a deposit decision, but a clear handover-focused checklist helps reduce avoidable cleaning issues.

Yes. Oven cleaning and carpet cleaning can be quoted as extras before booking.
