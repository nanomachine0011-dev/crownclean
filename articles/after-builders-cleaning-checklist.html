After renovation or repair work, fine dust can settle on ledges, switches, floors and surfaces for days. This checklist helps Birmingham customers prepare for a more effective post-work clean.

When to book after builders cleaning

The best time to book is once the main trades have finished, tools are removed and the space is safe to clean. If work is still ongoing, a staged clean may be more realistic.

  • Wait until drilling, sanding and cutting have finished
  • Make sure water and electricity are available
  • Confirm safe access
  • Remove tools and loose materials
  • Share photos of dust level before quoting

After builders dust checklist

Builder dust moves into edges and high-touch areas. A clear checklist helps prevent obvious missed points.

  • Reachable ledges and sills
  • Skirting boards
  • Doors, frames and handles
  • Light switches and sockets
  • Kitchen and bathroom surfaces

Room-by-room post-work checklist

The exact scope depends on the work carried out, but these areas usually need attention after renovations.

  • Kitchen worktops, sink, cupboard fronts and appliance exteriors
  • Bathroom fixtures, mirrors, taps and tiles
  • Living spaces, hallways and stairs
  • Bedrooms and wardrobes exteriors
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped where suitable

What should be cleared before cleaning

After builders cleaning is not the same as trade waste removal. Clearing the space first gives the cleaning team more time for dust and details.

  • Rubble and trade waste
  • Paint tins and tools
  • Loose screws or sharp materials
  • Plastic sheeting where no longer needed
  • Items blocking floors or surfaces

FAQs

Can you clean while builders are still working?

It depends. If dust-producing work is still active, a final clean may need to wait or be split into stages.

Do you remove trade waste?

No. Trade waste and bulky waste should be cleared before the cleaning appointment.

Can carpets be cleaned after building work?

Yes, carpet cleaning can be quoted separately where suitable.
